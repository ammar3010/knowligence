Microsoft is a technology company focused on software,
cloud computing, artificial intelligence and enterprise
technology.

Microsoft operates Azure, a cloud computing platform used
by organizations around the world.

OpenAI develops artificial intelligence systems and large
language models.

Microsoft has invested in OpenAI and provides cloud
infrastructure for OpenAI.

Google operates Google Cloud and develops artificial
intelligence technologies.

Google and Microsoft compete across several areas of
cloud computing and artificial intelligence.

Amazon operates AWS, one of the largest cloud computing
platforms.

AWS competes with Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud.
